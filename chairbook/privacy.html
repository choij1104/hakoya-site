Chairbook — Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Chairbook is an appointment, client, and earnings book for stylists, published by HAKOYA LLC. This policy describes what data the app collects. The short answer is none.

What the app does with your data

Nothing. Chairbook does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you, your clients, or your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app's own storage on your device:

  • Appointments, services, prices, payments, and tips
  • Client names, phone numbers, and notes
  • Expenses and team member names

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. It is not accessible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Exports and backups

When you export a CSV file or a backup, the app opens the iOS share sheet and you choose where the file goes, such as Files, Mail, or AirDrop. We never receive these files.

Your clients' information

You are responsible for the client information you enter. Only record what you need, and delete client records you no longer need from the Clients screen or with "Erase all data" in Settings.

Network

None. Chairbook works entirely offline. All fonts and content ship inside the app, and the app makes no network requests.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children

Chairbook is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
