Foundation Forensics — Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy covers the Foundation Forensics app for iPhone and iPad, published by HAKOYA LLC. Foundation Forensics is a field tool for documenting residential foundation evaluations. HAKOYA LLC does not collect any information about you, your clients, or the properties you evaluate.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting. HAKOYA LLC has no server that receives your data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app's storage on your device:

  • Firm and inspector details, including your logo, seal, and signature images
  • Project details: property address, client name and contact information, and report fields
  • Elevation measurements, plan views, plumbing data, observations, site photos, and attached evidence files

This information leaves your device only when you choose to export it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera and photo library access only so you can add site photos, floor plans, and your logo, seal, and signature to a report. It reads only the images you choose.

Optional soil and location lookup

If you use the optional soil lookup, the app sends the property address you entered to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoding service to get its coordinates, then sends those coordinates to the USDA Soil Data Access service to retrieve published soil data. These requests go directly from your device to those public government services. HAKOYA LLC does not receive them. The lookup runs only when you start it. Each service's own privacy policy applies to the request.

Exports, reports, and backups

When you save a PDF report, a project file, or a backup, the app opens the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes, such as Files, Mail, or AirDrop. We never receive these files.

Subscription

Foundation Forensics Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID.

Your clients' information

You are responsible for the client and property information you record and share. Record only what your evaluation needs, and delete projects you no longer need.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software.

Children

Foundation Forensics is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
