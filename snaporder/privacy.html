Snaporder — Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Snaporder turns screenshots of order conversations into an order list for small sellers. It is published by HAKOYA LLC. This policy describes what data the app collects. The short answer is none.

What the app does with your data

Nothing. Snaporder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you, your customers, or your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

Screenshots, photos, and the camera

You choose which screenshots or photos to add, or you take a photo with the camera. The app reads the text in each image with Apple's on-device text recognition (the Vision framework). The image and the recognized text are processed only on your iPhone and are never uploaded. The app asks for camera and photo library access only to read the images you pick, and it never reads anything else in your library.

Data stored on your device

Everything you save stays in the app's own storage on your device:

  • Orders: items, prices, quantities, pickup dates, status, and payment method
  • Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, and notes taken from your screenshots or typed in
  • The screenshots you attach to orders, and your shop name and item list

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. It is not accessible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Exports and backups

When you export a CSV file or a backup, the app opens the iOS share sheet and you choose where the file goes, such as Files, Mail, or AirDrop. We never receive these files.

Your customers' information

You are responsible for the customer information you save. Only keep what you need to fill orders, and delete orders you no longer need, or use "Erase all data" in Settings.

Network

None. Snaporder works entirely offline. Text recognition runs on the device, all fonts and content ship inside the app, and the app makes no network requests.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. Snaporder is not affiliated with Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or any messaging service.

Children

Snaporder is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
