Clinical and field medicine

TOXCARD

Emergency toxicology reference

An offline emergency toxicology reference for trained clinicians. Every entry gives the toxin, the agent, the dose exactly as its source states it, and the pitfall that decides the outcome.

187 toxin entries in 24 categories, 79 antidote and adjunct agents, and 8 toxidromes. Every entry carries an evidence tier and at least one named source.

Coming to iPhone on the App Store.

Silent Medic

Offline decision support for prolonged field care

Offline clinical decision support for prolonged field care. A query console searches by drug, symptom, or mechanism and returns citation-linked answers.

Built on the TCCC-RX knowledge base (113 drugs, 440 dual-use mappings). It is a deterministic rule base, not a language model, and makes no network calls.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.

TCCC Field

Point-of-injury casualty care

Offline browser-based decision support for Tactical Combat Casualty Care at the point of injury, with training simulation and a command-level module for managing multiple casualties.

Casualty records move from the field module to the command dashboard as structured data.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.

LONESTAR

Rural prehospital emergency guide for Texas

An emergency guide for rural Texas, written for people who may be the first on scene before professional help arrives.

Available in English and Spanish.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.

Silent Medic Vet

Veterinary field reference

A veterinary field reference covering thirteen species, with separate modes for animal owners and for veterinarians.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.

Everyday life

HomeRx

Over-the-counter medicine and supplement reference

A reference for U.S. over-the-counter medicines and dietary supplements, written for immigrants, older adults, and community organizations. It helps people ask safer questions at the pharmacy; it is not diagnosis or prescribing.

114 entries across 14 categories, in English, Korean, and Japanese.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.

Mind Companion

Daily companion for older adults

A simple daily companion for older adults: mood check-ins, memory aids, and medicine check-offs, with large type and voice input.

Works offline. It is a personal memory aid, not a medical device.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.

Field and business

Foundation Forensics

Residential foundation evaluation and reporting

Documentation for ASCE Level B residential foundation evaluations: multi-visit monitoring, field-test evidence for each segment, and raw-data export for review by a licensed professional engineer.

It produces technical documentation only; it does not give an engineering opinion.

For availability, contact info@hakoyallc.com.
