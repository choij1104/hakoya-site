TOXCARD — Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

TOXCARD is a clinical toxicology reference published by HAKOYA LLC. This policy describes what data the app collects. The short answer is none.

What the app does with your data

Nothing. TOXCARD does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you or your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few conveniences in its own local storage on your device:

  • Your saved (starred) entries
  • Recently viewed entries
  • Your theme preference

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

TOXCARD works entirely offline, and all reference content ships inside the app. When the device is online, the app may request display fonts from Google Fonts; that request sends no information you enter into the app.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children

TOXCARD is not directed at children. It is intended for trained healthcare professionals.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
