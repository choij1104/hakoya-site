TOXCARD

Toxin · Agent · Pitfall — an offline emergency toxicology reference for trained clinicians.

Coming to iPhone on the App Store. Published by HAKOYA LLC.

What it is

Every entry is built the same way: the toxin, the agent, the dose as its source states it, and the pitfall — the timing error, missed endpoint, or contraindication that turns a treatable poisoning into a death. The pitfall shares the screen with the dose.

  • 187 toxin entries across 24 categories (dataset 2026.09.17)
  • 79 antidote and adjunct agents, each linked to every toxin it treats
  • 8 toxidromes, each naming the finding that separates it from its look-alike
  • An evidence tier and at least one named source on every entry
  • Works offline from first launch. No account. No data collected.

TOXCARD is a clinical reference for trained healthcare professionals. It does not diagnose, it is not a treatment protocol, and it does not replace clinical judgment or a regional poison center. Doses are shown exactly as their sources state them; confirm every dose against your own formulary before administration. In the United States, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.
